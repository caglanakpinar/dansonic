How the sounds play (beat mode)

The song's vocal goes the same way in every bar. A hit (a swipe of an arm, or the start of a pose) plays the sound of the beat of the bar it lands on, at most one per beat. Holding a pose keeps the line going on every beat, and so does any movement in the picture, even a dancer the pose tracker does not see or a move that is no known gesture; a still picture is silent. The three phrases have their own moves and are not cut while they play.

Beat 1 is sung two ways. In most bars it is one ab; in bars 1, 3 and 8 of the video it is ab ab over beats 1 and 2. A hit on beat 1 plays ab at once. If both arms hit within 0.25 s of it (hands to the chest, both arms up, a left and a right swipe together), the second ab follows on beat 2 in place of been: together they are the ab-ab sound (group in config.yaml).

The dance opens with ab ab: nothing plays until that move (both hands on the chest, opening in config.yaml) is seen, and it then becomes beat 1 of the bar. The opening is forgiving: one clear frame of the move is enough, freer versions (hands lower or crossed a little, both forearms up) count when held for 0.3 s, and the K key teaches the app your own ab ab. Restarting the loop or the video closes that gate again, and so does the end of the senkron (10 bars, senkron in config.yaml); the O key opens it by hand. Before the dance (the senkron) starts only the individual motions sound (a hand up plays ab, individual in config.yaml); they do not start it, and once it has started they are silent.

The loop's first beat is beat 1. Press R when the dance starts, or B on the dancer's 1, to line the bar up. The old way, one move per sound, is in config.gestures.yaml.
